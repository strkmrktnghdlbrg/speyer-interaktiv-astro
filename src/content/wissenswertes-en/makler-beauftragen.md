When selling a home, emotional ties usually play a large role. After all, you have enjoyed your home here for many years and want security for yourself and your family. If you are moving to another city or abroad, the sale will likely be one of the most important financial decisions of your life.

To make that decision well, it is important to get a clear picture of your property's actual value. The best way is the objective eye of a professional - a formal valuation should definitely be commissioned. A local provider is the straightforward route to finding the right agent.

A professional agent knows current property prices in the region, knows which apartment sells at which price - whether in the [Old Town](/en/districts/old-town/) or at the [Rhine harbor](/en/districts/new-rhine-harbor/) - and can help you sell your property at the best possible price. In negotiations with prospective buyers, the agent is an expert counterpart who can spare you a great deal of work and trouble.

## The advantages of an agent

A good agent knows not only the local market but also the legal framework. That saves you time and effort when searching for the right property. They help you find the best offer and negotiate a good price with the seller.

Many sellers first ask themselves whether they need an agent at all. The answer is usually yes. Only a professional real estate agent has the know-how and the contacts to market your property successfully. On average, an agent finds a buyer for a house or apartment within about six weeks. Of course, it depends on the market. In large cities like Berlin, Hamburg or Munich, the market is very active with many potential buyers. In a mid-sized city like Speyer, good market knowledge is especially valuable.

A good agent also has a wide network of potential buyers and sellers. Their experience yields valuable advice for the selling or buying process, and they can mediate between the different needs of buyer and seller. They are a reliable point of contact for all questions around a property sale - from finding the right financing partner to concluding the notarized purchase contract.

## Why agents matter right now

Rising prices are both a blessing and a curse for agents. On the one hand revenues rise, because more people try to invest in property. On the other hand, the costs of advertising and marketing to reach new clients rise too. In the age of online advertising, winning the attention of potential clients has become harder. Good agents have to be clever and find the right mix of offline and online marketing.

For sellers and buyers this means: having a competent agent at your side is a clear advantage. Especially where supply is limited, as is often the case in Speyer, experience, network and negotiating skill are decisive. ## Frequently asked questions

### Do I absolutely need an agent to sell?

It is not mandatory, but a good agent saves a lot of effort and often achieves a better price. Especially in emotionally charged sales, the outside perspective is valuable.

### How long does a sale take?

On average, an agent finds a buyer within six weeks. Market conditions and the property itself play a major role, though.

### What should I look for when choosing an agent?

Local market knowledge, a good network, experience with comparable properties and transparent terms.
